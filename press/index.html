Story angles

Every escape has a price

Six archdemons sit caged on planets that orbit Lucifer. Let one break out and its sin turns against you for the rest of the stage: Greed cuts your faith regeneration, Gluttony makes every bar fill faster, Sloth makes surges hit harder. Each mistake changes how you play.

Real demons, real sources

Leviathan, Mammon, Asmodeus, Satan, Beelzebub and Belphegor each carry one deadly sin, as in Peter Binsfeld's 1589 classification. Lucifer, Pride, is the boss. The relics are biblical too: Nehushtan, the Seal of Solomon, the Mantle of Elijah.

A mobile roguelite where you actually play

No auto-battling. Every push-back is your own tap or hold. Release a hold at the planet's sweet spot for a PERFECT: it costs less faith and builds a streak. One finger, real timing.

Honest free-to-play

Best moments, and how to reach them

  1. PERFECT streaks. Hold a planet and let go when the marker sits in the bright sweet spot. Each planet has its own timing, from 0.8 s on the Moon to 2.0 s on Saturn. Chain them and the streak multiplier climbs.
  2. Surges. Lucifer throws a glowing orb that flies for six seconds before it slams a planet's bar. Watch it wind up, then answer the hit with a PERFECT hold.
  3. A breakout. Let one bar fill to the top. The demon smashes its cage, flies to the sin bar at the top of the screen, and its sin turns on for the rest of the stage.
  4. Jupiter's inhale. Pick the Gluttony stage before a level. Every 20 seconds Jupiter pulls bar from every other planet, then gulps it down.
  5. Blessings. Fire of the Heavens hits every planet at once: use it when all the bars are high. Avatar of the Creator makes your next holds three times as strong.
  6. Lucifer. Stage four of every run. He summons Earth for the Doomsday Ritual: hold Earth to break it before the timer ends. From Ascension 1 he also charges a Soul Blast.
  7. Relic reveals. Open a relic pack in the shop. Epic and legendary relics get the biggest reveal.

Description, 500 words

Demon Orbit is a one-finger angel roguelite for Android, built solo in Godot by a game director with about twenty years in the games industry. It asks one question again and again: can you let go at exactly the right moment?

Six archdemons sit caged on the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter and Saturn, and the planets orbit Lucifer himself. Each demon carries one deadly sin: Leviathan is Envy, Mammon is Greed, Asmodeus is Lust, Satan is Wrath, Beelzebub is Gluttony and Belphegor is Sloth. Lucifer is Pride, and he is the boss. The pairings follow Peter Binsfeld's classification from 1589.

Every cage has a breakout bar that fills over time, and Lucifer speeds it up with basic attacks and surges: glowing orbs that wind up, rush across the screen and slam a planet's bar after a six-second flight.

You do it with one finger. A tap gives a small push-back. A hold gives a big one, and the length of the hold matters. Every planet has its own sweet spot, from 0.8 seconds on the Moon to 2.0 seconds on Saturn. Release in that window for a PERFECT: it costs less faith, pushes harder and builds a streak. Every action costs faith, so sloppy timing drains you fast.

When a bar reaches the top, the demon breaks out. It smashes its cage, flies to the sin bar at the top of the screen, and its sin stays active for the rest of the stage. Greed cuts your faith regeneration, Lust makes every action cost more faith, Gluttony makes every bar fill faster, and Sloth makes surges hit harder. One mistake changes how the rest of the stage plays.

A run has four stages. Before each of the first three, you choose which sin to unleash, and that sin shapes the whole stage: in a Gluttony stage, Jupiter pulls bar from every other planet every twenty seconds; in a Wrath stage, Lucifer's basic attacks also feed Mars. The fourth stage is Lucifer, who adds his own abilities, including the Doomsday Ritual: he summons Earth, and you must hold it until the ritual breaks.

Between stages you build your run. There are ten biblical relics, such as the Crown of Thorns, the Shroud of Turin, Nehushtan, the Seal of Solomon and the Spear of Longinus. Each relic carries one of seven virtues that counters a sin, and comes in five rarities. You also take two of four blessings into each run: Drop of Miracles restores faith, Fire of the Heavens burns every bar back, Seraphim's Wing wards against surges, and Avatar of the Creator makes your next holds three times as strong. Each blessing has three variants.

Outside the runs, the Sanctuary holds permanent upgrades, and five Ascension levels raise the difficulty for experienced players.

Demon Orbit is free to play. There is no energy system. Ads are optional, only when you choose to watch one for a bonus, and purchases are optional too. The core game plays offline. It is available in English and Brazilian Portuguese, and the Android soft launch is coming soon.
